# Nerve Condition Images

Illustrations of nerve-related conditions showing nerve impingement, compression, and neuropathy.

## Purpose

Show patients how nerves are compressed or irritated, causing radiculopathy and neuropathic pain. Illustrate nerve compression points and anatomical causes.

## Image Types

- Nerve root compression
- Nerve impingement at neural foramen
- Nerve entrapment (peripheral)
- Radiculopathy presentation
- Neuropathy (peripheral, proximal)
- Nerve inflammation
- Nerve injury progression
- Nerve conduction disruption
- CRPS/RSD illustrations
- Complex regional pain syndrome stages

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Colors:** Color-coded nerves, red for inflammation
- **Labeling:** Nerve names and compression sites

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[region]-[nerve]-[condition].webp`

**Examples:**

- `lumbar-nerve-compression.webp` - Nerve root compressed by disc
- `l5-nerve-root-impingement.webp` - Specific level compression
- `cervical-nerve-entrapment.webp` - Cervical radiculopathy
- `peripheral-neuropathy-foot.webp` - Neuropathic foot condition
- `sciatic-nerve-entrapment.webp` - Sciatic nerve at various points
- `ulnar-nerve-entrapment.webp` - Upper extremity neuropathy
- `complex-regional-pain-syndrome.webp` - CRPS illustration
- `reflex-sympathetic-dystrophy.webp` - RSD stages
- `nerve-inflammation.webp` - Neuritis illustration

## Usage

Used on:
- Lumbar radiculopathy pages
- Cervical radiculopathy pages
- Neuropathy pages
- Peripheral nerve pages
- CRPS/RSD pages
- Radicular pain education
