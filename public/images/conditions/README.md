# Condition Images

Visual representations of pain conditions, showing how conditions affect anatomy and patient experience.

## Overview

This folder contains illustrations that explain various pain conditions to patients. Images may show pathology, progression, or clinical presentation of different conditions affecting the spine, nerves, joints, and head.

## Subfolders

- **spine/** - Spine-related conditions (stenosis, radiculopathy, disc herniation, etc.)
- **nerve/** - Nerve-related conditions (nerve impingement, neuropathy, etc.)
- **joint/** - Joint conditions (osteoarthritis, facet syndrome, etc.)
- **headache/** - Headache and cervicogenic conditions

## Global Standards

- **Format:** WEBP (preferred) or PNG (fallback)
- **Naming:** lowercase, hyphen-separated, condition-specific
- **Style:** Professional medical illustration showing pathology
- **Accuracy:** Reviewed for clinical accuracy

## General Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Resolution:** 72-150 DPI
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Comparison:** Many show healthy vs. affected side-by-side
