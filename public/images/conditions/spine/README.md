# Spine Condition Images

Illustrations of spine-related conditions showing pathology, progression, and clinical presentations.

## Purpose

Show patients what is happening in their spine when they have conditions like disc herniation, spinal stenosis, spondylolisthesis, and degenerative disc disease. Used on spine condition evaluation pages.

## Image Types

- Disc herniation (normal vs. herniated comparison)
- Bulging disc
- Disc extrusion
- Spinal stenosis (central, lateral, foraminal)
- Spondylolisthesis (grades I-IV)
- Degenerative disc disease progression
- Facet hypertrophy
- Ligamentum flavum thickening
- Listhesis and instability
- Post-surgical changes
- Persistent pain after surgery

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 900x700px to 1200x900px (often cross-sectional)
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Style:** Show pathology clearly with labels
- **Comparison:** Often show healthy vs. affected

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[region]-[condition]-[detail].webp`

**Examples:**

- `lumbar-disc-herniation.webp` - Herniated disc illustration
- `lumbar-disc-bulge-vs-normal.webp` - Comparison image
- `lumbar-spinal-stenosis-cross-section.webp` - Cross-sectional view
- `lumbar-spondylolisthesis-grade-2.webp` - Grade II listhesis
- `degenerative-disc-disease-progression.webp` - Multi-stage illustration
- `cervical-stenosis-foraminal.webp` - Foraminal narrowing
- `thoracic-facet-hypertrophy.webp` - Facet arthritis
- `lumbar-persistent-pain-after-fusion.webp` - Post-surgical pathology
- `disc-extrusion-vs-protrusion.webp` - Disc pathology comparison

## Usage

Used on:
- Low back pain evaluation pages
- Neck pain evaluation pages
- Lumbar radiculopathy pages
- Cervical radiculopathy pages
- Spinal stenosis pages
- Persistent pain after surgery pages
- Patient education hub
