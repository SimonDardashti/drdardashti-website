# Joint Condition Images

Illustrations of joint diseases and conditions affecting joints throughout the body.

## Purpose

Show patients how osteoarthritis, facet syndrome, and other joint conditions develop and progress. Illustrate joint space narrowing, cartilage loss, and inflammatory changes.

## Image Types

- Osteoarthritis progression
- Facet syndrome and hypertrophy
- Cartilage degradation
- Bone spur formation
- Synovitis and joint inflammation
- Joint effusion
- Labral tears
- Ligament injuries
- Bursa inflammation
- Rheumatoid arthritis changes
- Post-traumatic arthritis

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Progression:** Often shown in stages
- **Comparison:** Healthy vs. arthritic side-by-side

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[joint]-[condition]-[stage].webp`

**Examples:**

- `facet-osteoarthritis-progression.webp` - Multi-stage progression
- `knee-osteoarthritis-stage-2.webp` - Specific stage
- `hip-osteoarthritis.webp` - Hip joint arthritis
- `shoulder-labral-tear.webp` - Labrum pathology
- `facet-joint-arthritis.webp` - Spine facet arthritis
- `sacroiliac-joint-arthritis.webp` - SI joint arthritis
- `cartilage-loss-timeline.webp` - Cartilage degeneration
- `bone-spur-formation.webp` - Osteophyte development
- `inflammatory-joint-arthritis.webp` - Inflammatory conditions

## Usage

Used on:
- Joint pain evaluation pages
- Facet-mediated pain pages
- Sacroiliac joint pages
- Osteoarthritis pages
- Arthritis education
- Conservative care pages
