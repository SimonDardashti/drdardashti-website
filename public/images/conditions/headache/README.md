# Headache Condition Images

Illustrations of headache types and cervicogenic headache mechanisms.

## Purpose

Show patients the anatomical basis for headaches, particularly cervicogenic headache arising from cervical spine and neck structures.

## Image Types

- Cervicogenic headache pathway
- Cervical referred pain distribution
- Tension headache muscle involvement
- Occipital neuralgia nerve path
- Migraine anatomy
- Trigeminovascular system
- Cervical spine referral patterns
- Neck muscle tension
- Suboccipital region anatomy
- Greater occipital nerve pathway

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Labeling:** Nerve and muscle pathways clearly marked
- **Pain patterns:** Show referred pain distribution areas

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[headache-type]-[detail].webp`

**Examples:**

- `cervicogenic-headache-anatomy.webp` - Cervical origin of headache
- `cervicogenic-headache-referral-pattern.webp` - Pain distribution
- `greater-occipital-nerve-pathway.webp` - Occipital neuralgia
- `tension-headache-muscles.webp` - Muscle-tension headache
- `cervical-radiculopathy-headache.webp` - Cervical radicular headache
- `occipital-nerve-compression.webp` - Occipital neuralgia mechanism
- `cervical-facet-referred-pain.webp` - Facet-mediated headache
- `migraine-trigeminovascular.webp` - Migraine mechanism
- `whiplash-headache-pathology.webp` - Post-whiplash headache

## Usage

Used on:
- Cervicogenic headache pages
- Neck pain evaluation pages
- Whiplash pages
- Headache condition pages
- Conservative care for headache
- Patient education
