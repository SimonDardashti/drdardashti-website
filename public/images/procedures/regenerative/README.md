# Regenerative Procedure Images

Illustrations of regenerative medicine procedures including PRP and stem cell therapies.

## Purpose

Show patients how regenerative procedures work - how biologics are processed and injected to promote tissue healing.

## Image Types

- Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) processing
- PRP injection technique
- Stem cell collection
- Stem cell processing
- Stem cell injection
- Biological healing cascade
- Tendon healing with PRP
- Cartilage regeneration
- Ligament healing
- Disc regeneration
- Growth factor mechanisms
- Cell differentiation

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 900x700px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Process:** Show collection through application
- **Cellular:** Illustrate biological processes when relevant
- **Timeline:** Show healing progression

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[therapy-type]-[application]-[detail].webp`

**Examples:**

- `platelet-rich-plasma-prp-processing.webp` - PRP preparation
- `prp-injection-knee.webp` - PRP knee injection
- `prp-tendon-healing.webp` - Tendon regeneration
- `prp-cartilage-regeneration.webp` - Cartilage healing
- `stem-cell-processing.webp` - Cell preparation
- `stem-cell-injection.webp` - Cell injection technique
- `stem-cell-cartilage-healing.webp` - Cartilage regeneration
- `biological-healing-cascade.webp` - Cellular process
- `growth-factor-mechanism.webp` - Biological mechanism

## Usage

Used on:
- PRP injection pages
- Stem cell procedure pages
- Regenerative medicine pages
- Advanced treatment options
- Tissue healing education
