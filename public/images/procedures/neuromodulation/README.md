# Neuromodulation Procedure Images

Illustrations of spinal cord stimulation and peripheral nerve stimulation systems.

## Purpose

Show patients how neuromodulation devices work - how implanted electrodes deliver electrical signals to interrupt pain pathways.

## Image Types

- Spinal cord stimulation (SCS) system
- SCS trial procedure
- SCS permanent implant
- Electrode paddle placement
- Lead positioning in epidural space
- Pulse generator implant site
- Wireless and rechargeable systems
- Peripheral nerve stimulation
- Occipital nerve stimulation
- Device programming
- Pain gate control mechanism
- Multi-electrode arrays

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 900x700px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Device:** Show implanted hardware realistically
- **Placement:** Clear anatomical positioning
- **Electrical:** Illustrate signal pathways

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[scs-or-device]-[type]-[detail].webp`

**Examples:**

- `spinal-cord-stimulation-system.webp` - Complete SCS overview
- `scs-electrode-placement.webp` - Lead positioning
- `scs-trial-procedure.webp` - Trial setup
- `scs-pulse-generator-implant.webp` - IPG implantation
- `scs-pain-gate-mechanism.webp` - How SCS works
- `peripheral-nerve-stimulation.webp` - PNS device
- `occipital-nerve-stimulation.webp` - Occipital lead
- `scs-wireless-system.webp` - Wireless device
- `scs-electrode-paddle.webp` - Paddle array

## Usage

Used on:
- Spinal cord stimulation evaluation pages
- SCS trial pages
- Neuromodulation pages
- Advanced treatment options
- Chronic pain management pages
