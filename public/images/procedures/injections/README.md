# Injection Procedure Images

Step-by-step illustrations of injection procedures used for diagnosis and treatment.

## Purpose

Show patients how various injection procedures are performed, where medication is placed, and how it works. Reduce procedural anxiety through visualization.

## Image Types

- Epidural steroid injection (lumbar, thoracic, cervical)
- Facet joint injection
- Medial branch block
- Sacroiliac joint injection
- Trigger point injection
- Peripheral nerve block
- Joint injection (knee, hip, shoulder)
- Bursa injection
- Tendon sheath injection
- Needle placement anatomy
- Medication distribution
- Imaging guidance (fluoroscopy, ultrasound)

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 900x700px to 1200x900px (often taller for step-by-step)
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Steps:** Show 3-5 steps of procedure flow
- **Anatomy:** Clear landmark identification
- **Needle:** Show needle pathway and final position

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[procedure-name]-[detail]-[step].webp`

**Examples:**

- `epidural-steroid-injection-lumbar.webp` - Overview of procedure
- `lumbar-epidural-needle-placement.webp` - Final needle position
- `epidural-steroid-injection-step-1.webp` - First step
- `facet-joint-injection-technique.webp` - Facet injection
- `medial-branch-block-anatomy.webp` - MBB needle placement
- `sacroiliac-joint-injection.webp` - SI joint injection
- `trigger-point-injection-needle.webp` - Trigger point technique
- `peripheral-nerve-block-landmark.webp` - Nerve block anatomy
- `joint-injection-ultrasound-guided.webp` - US-guided technique

## Usage

Used on:
- Procedure evaluation pages
- Epidural steroid injection pages
- Facet joint injection pages
- Radiofrequency ablation pages
- Patient education for procedures
