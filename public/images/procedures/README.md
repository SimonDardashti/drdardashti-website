# Procedure Images

Illustrations of interventional and therapeutic procedures showing technique, needle placement, and expected outcomes.

## Overview

This folder contains step-by-step illustrations and technical diagrams of medical procedures. Images show needle placement under imaging guidance, medication delivery, and therapeutic effects.

## Subfolders

- **injections/** - Steroid and therapeutic injections
- **ablation/** - Radiofrequency and thermal ablation techniques
- **neuromodulation/** - Spinal cord stimulation, peripheral nerve stimulation
- **regenerative/** - PRP, stem cell, and regenerative medicine procedures

## Global Standards

- **Format:** WEBP (preferred) or PNG (fallback)
- **Naming:** lowercase, hyphen-separated, procedure-specific
- **Style:** Clear technical illustration showing needle/device placement
- **Accuracy:** Reviewed for technical accuracy

## General Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Resolution:** 72-150 DPI
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Step-by-step:** Multi-step procedures shown progressively
- **Anatomy:** Show relevant anatomical landmarks
