# Ablation Procedure Images

Illustrations of thermal and radiofrequency ablation procedures targeting nerves and tissues.

## Purpose

Show patients how ablation works - how radiofrequency energy creates lesions on pain-conducting nerves to provide pain relief.

## Image Types

- Radiofrequency ablation (RFA)
- Medial branch radiofrequency ablation
- Peripheral nerve radiofrequency ablation
- Medial branch lesioning technique
- Temperature zones during ablation
- Ablation electrode placement
- Thermal lesion formation
- Multi-electrode ablation
- Cooled radiofrequency ablation
- Pulsed radiofrequency
- Step-by-step ablation procedure

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 900x700px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Heat zones:** Show temperature gradients if applicable
- **Lesion:** Illustrate tissue changes from ablation
- **Guidance:** Show fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[ablation-type]-[target]-[detail].webp`

**Examples:**

- `radiofrequency-ablation-medial-branch.webp` - RFA overview
- `medial-branch-lesioning-technique.webp` - Electrode placement
- `radiofrequency-thermal-lesion.webp` - Heat zone illustration
- `cooled-radiofrequency-ablation.webp` - Cooled RFA technique
- `peripheral-nerve-ablation.webp` - Nerve ablation
- `pulsed-radiofrequency.webp` - Pulsed RF technique
- `ablation-electrode-positioning.webp` - Electrode placement
- `radiofrequency-ablation-pain-relief.webp` - Expected outcome
- `rfa-temperature-zones.webp` - Thermal distribution

## Usage

Used on:
- Radiofrequency ablation pages
- Medial branch ablation pages
- Peripheral nerve ablation pages
- Advanced procedure education
- Treatment option pages
