# Comparison Images

Side-by-side and before/after comparative illustrations showing healthy vs. affected anatomy.

## Purpose

Show patients visual comparisons between healthy and affected structures, or before/after treatment outcomes.

## Image Types

- Healthy vs. herniated disc
- Normal vs. stenotic spinal canal
- Healthy vs. arthritic joint
- Before vs. after exercise
- Normal vs. nerve-compressed
- Healthy vs. inflamed tissue
- Before vs. after treatment
- Good vs. poor posture
- Correct vs. incorrect form
- Normal nerve function vs. impingement
- Healthy vs. degenerated cartilage

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 1000x600px to 1400x800px (wider for side-by-side)
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Layout:** Side-by-side comparison preferred
- **Labeling:** Clear labels for each side
- **Contrast:** Visual distinction between healthy/affected
- **Color:** Use green for healthy, red/orange for affected

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[structure]-healthy-vs-[condition].webp`

**Examples:**

- `disc-healthy-vs-herniated.webp` - Disc comparison
- `spine-normal-vs-stenotic.webp` - Stenosis comparison
- `joint-healthy-vs-arthritic.webp` - Arthritis comparison
- `nerve-normal-vs-compressed.webp` - Compression comparison
- `posture-good-vs-poor.webp` - Posture comparison
- `exercise-correct-vs-incorrect.webp` - Form comparison
- `cartilage-healthy-vs-degenerated.webp` - Cartilage loss
- `before-after-treatment.webp` - Treatment results
- `tissue-healthy-vs-inflamed.webp` - Inflammation

## Usage

Used on:
- Condition evaluation pages
- Before/after procedure outcomes pages
- Exercise form pages
- Posture education pages
- Treatment results pages
- Patient education hub
