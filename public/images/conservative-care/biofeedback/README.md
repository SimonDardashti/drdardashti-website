# Biofeedback Images

Photographs and illustrations of biofeedback equipment and techniques for pain management.

## Purpose

Show patients how biofeedback helps them learn to self-regulate physical responses to reduce pain and stress.

## Image Types

- Biofeedback equipment setup
- Heart rate variability monitor
- Muscle tension biofeedback
- Skin temperature biofeedback
- Respiratory biofeedback
- Patient using biofeedback device
- Biofeedback training session
- Graphical feedback display
- Real-time monitoring
- Relaxation response measurement
- Thermal biofeedback
- EMG (electromyography) sensors

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** Clinical or training setting
- **Equipment:** Clear view of devices and displays
- **Model:** Patient using biofeedback
- **Display:** Show feedback screen/readings clearly
- **Focus:** Show patient learning process

## Naming Convention

Structure: `biofeedback-[type]-[view].webp`

**Examples:**

- `biofeedback-equipment-setup.webp` - Equipment overview
- `biofeedback-heart-rate.webp` - HRV biofeedback
- `biofeedback-muscle-tension.webp` - EMG sensors
- `biofeedback-training-session.webp` - Patient in training
- `biofeedback-display.webp` - Feedback display
- `biofeedback-relaxation.webp` - Relaxation training
- `biofeedback-breathing.webp` - Respiratory training
- `thermal-biofeedback.webp` - Temperature feedback
- `biofeedback-pain-management.webp` - Pain reduction benefit

## Usage

Used on:
- Conservative care pages
- Biofeedback therapy pages
- Self-regulation and pain management pages
- Patient education hub
