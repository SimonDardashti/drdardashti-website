# Exercise Images

Demonstrations of specific exercises for pain management, particularly spinal stability exercises.

## Purpose

Show patients proper form for exercises like McGill Big 3, core strengthening, and functional movement patterns.

## Image Types

- Curl-ups (McGill Big 3)
- Side planks (McGill Big 3)
- Bird dogs (McGill Big 3)
- Dead bugs
- Bridges
- Hip marches
- Quadruped rocking
- Supine marching
- Standing balance exercises
- Hip flexor stretches
- Hamstring stretches
- Quadriceps stretches
- Spinal mobility exercises
- Posture demonstration
- Before and after form comparison

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** Clean, neutral background
- **Model:** Professional demonstration of correct form
- **Views:** Front, side, and sometimes overhead views
- **Sequence:** Multi-image sequence for complex exercises
- **Focus:** Clear view of key body positions

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[exercise-name]-[position]-[view].webp`

**Examples:**

- `mcgill-big-3-curl-up.webp` - Curl-up exercise
- `mcgill-big-3-side-plank.webp` - Side plank exercise
- `mcgill-big-3-bird-dog.webp` - Bird dog exercise
- `side-plank-proper-form.webp` - Correct positioning
- `dead-bug-exercise.webp` - Dead bug movement
- `glute-bridge.webp` - Bridge exercise
- `quadruped-rocking.webp` - Quadruped exercise
- `hamstring-stretch-standing.webp` - Stretching
- `posture-correct-vs-slouch.webp` - Postural comparison

## Usage

- Exercise demonstration pages
