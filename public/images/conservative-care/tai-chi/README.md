# Tai Chi Images

Photographs of tai chi poses and movements for pain management and balance.

## Purpose

Show patients tai chi poses and movements used for balance, flexibility, and pain management.

## Image Types

- Tai chi starting position
- Tai chi basic movements
- Weight shifting
- Balance poses
- Standing postures
- Flowing movements
- Group tai chi class
- Outdoor tai chi practice
- Balance improvement
- Posture during tai chi
- Step-by-step movement sequence

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** Neutral or outdoor nature setting
- **Model:** Professional tai chi instructor demonstration
- **Clothing:** Comfortable, loose clothing
- **Sequence:** Multi-frame sequences for complex moves
- **Clarity:** Full body view of positions

## Naming Convention

Structure: `tai-chi-[movement]-[position].webp`

**Examples:**

- `tai-chi-starting-position.webp` - Starting pose
- `tai-chi-weight-shift.webp` - Weight transfer
- `tai-chi-standing-balance.webp` - Balance pose
- `tai-chi-flowing-movement.webp` - Flowing motion
- `tai-chi-class.webp` - Group practice
- `tai-chi-outdoor.webp` - Outdoor practice
- `tai-chi-arm-movement.webp` - Arm positions
- `tai-chi-stepping-pattern.webp` - Stepping sequence
- `tai-chi-pain-management.webp` - Therapeutic benefit

## Usage

Used on:
- Conservative care pages
- Pain management education
- Balance and fall prevention pages
- Tai chi therapy pages
- Patient education hub
