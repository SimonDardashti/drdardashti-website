# Physical Therapy Images

Photographs of physical therapy techniques, modalities, and treatment settings.

## Purpose

Show patients the various PT techniques used for pain management and functional restoration.

## Image Types

- Manual therapy techniques
- Soft tissue mobilization
- Joint mobilization
- Stretching demonstrations
- Resistance training
- Therapeutic exercise with equipment
- PT clinic setting
- Equipment setup
- Patient-therapist interaction
- Range of motion assessment
- Posture correction
- Functional movement training

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** PT clinic or neutral setting
- **Model:** Professional demonstration
- **Hands:** Clear view of therapist hand placement
- **Equipment:** Show PT equipment clearly
- **Multiple angles:** Different perspectives of techniques

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[therapy-type]-[body-region].webp`

**Examples:**

- `manual-therapy-lumbar-spine.webp` - Manual technique
- `soft-tissue-mobilization.webp` - Soft tissue work
- `joint-mobilization-lumbar.webp` - Joint mobilization
- `resistance-training-with-band.webp` - Resistance exercise
- `physical-therapy-clinic.webp` - Clinic setting
- `range-of-motion-assessment.webp` - Assessment
- `posture-correction-technique.webp` - Posture work
- `functional-movement-training.webp` - Functional training
- `therapeutic-stretching.webp` - Stretching technique

## Usage

Used on:
- Conservative care pages
- Physical therapy education
- Treatment option pages
- Patient education hub
