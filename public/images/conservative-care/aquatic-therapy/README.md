# Aquatic Therapy Images

Photographs of water-based exercises and aquatic physical therapy settings.

## Purpose

Show patients how aquatic therapy reduces weight-bearing stress while providing resistance for strengthening.

## Image Types

- Pool setting
- Deep water walking
- Shallow water exercises
- Water-based stretching
- Resistance exercises in water
- Flotation device use
- Buoyancy assistance
- Water current resistance
- Patient in pool
- Therapist-assisted aquatic exercise
- Temperature-controlled pool

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** Pool environment
- **Model:** Patient demonstrating exercises in water
- **Clarity:** Clear view of body position in water
- **Safety:** Show proper water depth and boundaries
- **Equipment:** Show flotation and resistance devices

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[exercise-type]-aquatic-[detail].webp`

**Examples:**

- `aquatic-therapy-pool.webp` - Pool setting
- `deep-water-walking.webp` - Walking exercise
- `shallow-water-exercise.webp` - Shallow water work
- `water-resistance-exercise.webp` - Resistance training
- `aquatic-stretching.webp` - Stretching in water
- `water-walking-pain-relief.webp` - Walking benefit
- `aquatic-therapy-buoyancy.webp` - Buoyancy assistance
- `pool-rehabilitation.webp` - Therapy session
- `water-exercise-low-impact.webp` - Low-impact exercise
