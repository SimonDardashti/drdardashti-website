# Conservative Care Images

Photographs and illustrations of conservative pain management techniques and therapies.

## Overview

This folder contains images of exercises, physical therapy techniques, and mind-body practices used in conservative pain management.

## Subfolders

- **exercises/** - Exercise demonstrations and form
- **physical-therapy/** - PT techniques and modalities
- **aquatic-therapy/** - Water-based therapy
- **tai-chi/** - Tai chi movements and poses
- **meditation/** - Meditation and mindfulness
- **biofeedback/** - Biofeedback techniques
- **cbt/** - Cognitive behavioral therapy illustrations

## Global Standards

- **Format:** WEBP (preferred) or PNG (fallback)
- **Naming:** lowercase, hyphen-separated, descriptive
- **Style:** Professional photographs or clear illustrations
- **Accuracy:** Proper form and technique demonstrated

## General Specifications

- **Dimensions:** Varies by type (often 800x600px to 1200x900px)
- **Resolution:** 72-150 DPI
- **Background:** Clean, neutral, or white
- **Model:** Professional demonstration of technique
- **Multiple angles:** Front, side views often needed
