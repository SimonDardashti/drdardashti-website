# Meditation Images

Photographs and illustrations of meditation and mindfulness practices for pain management.

## Purpose

Show patients meditation and mindfulness practices used to reduce pain perception and stress.

## Image Types

- Sitting meditation postures
- Guided meditation setting
- Breathing exercises
- Mindfulness illustration
- Quiet environment
- Group meditation
- Indoor meditation space
- Outdoor meditation
- Meditation cushion setup
- Pain management mindfulness
- Stress reduction visualization
- Body scan awareness

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** Peaceful, calm settings
- **Model:** Person meditating in calm pose
- **Lighting:** Soft, calming lighting
- **Atmosphere:** Convey peace and tranquility
- **Multiple settings:** Indoor and outdoor options

## Naming Convention

Structure: `meditation-[type]-[setting].webp`

**Examples:**

- `meditation-sitting-posture.webp` - Sitting pose
- `meditation-guided.webp` - Guided meditation
- `meditation-breathing-exercise.webp` - Breathing practice
- `meditation-mindfulness.webp` - Mindfulness practice
- `meditation-peaceful-space.webp` - Meditation environment
- `meditation-group-class.webp` - Group practice
- `meditation-outdoor.webp` - Outdoor meditation
- `meditation-pain-relief.webp` - Pain management benefit
- `meditation-stress-reduction.webp` - Stress management

## Usage

Used on:
- Conservative care pages
- Mindfulness and meditation pages
- Pain management education
- Stress reduction pages
- Patient education hub
