# Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Images

Illustrations of cognitive behavioral therapy concepts and pain management strategies.

## Purpose

Show patients how CBT helps them identify and change pain-related thoughts and behaviors to improve pain management.

## Image Types

- Thought-feeling-behavior loop
- Cognitive distortion examples
- Pain catastrophizing illustration
- Mindset shift diagram
- Coping strategy tools
- Problem-solving flowchart
- Relaxation techniques
- Pain-related anxiety cycle
- Behavioral activation
- Sleep hygiene concepts
- Stress management techniques
- Resilience building illustrations

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG (flowcharts SVG)
- **Background:** Clean, white or light background
- **Text:** Clear, readable text on diagrams
- **Flow:** Logical flow for learning concepts
- **Color-coding:** Use colors to distinguish concepts
- **Icons:** Simple icons to illustrate ideas

## Naming Convention

Structure: `cbt-[concept]-[detail].webp`

**Examples:**

- `cbt-thought-feeling-behavior-loop.webp` - Core CBT model
- `pain-catastrophizing.webp` - Catastrophic thinking
- `cognitive-distortions.webp` - Distorted thinking patterns
- `cbt-coping-strategies.webp` - Coping tools
- `pain-anxiety-cycle.webp` - Anxiety-pain loop
- `problem-solving-flowchart.webp` - Problem-solving steps
- `behavioral-activation.webp` - Activity scheduling
- `sleep-hygiene-tips.webp` - Sleep improvement
- `stress-management-techniques.webp` - Stress tools
- `mindset-pain-resilience.webp` - Growth mindset

## Usage

Used on:
- Conservative care pages
- CBT for pain pages
- Mental health and pain pages
- Patient education hub
- Pain psychology pages
