# Spine Anatomy Images

Detailed illustrations of spinal column anatomy for patient education and condition explanation.

## Purpose

Show patients the structure of their spine, including vertebrae, discs, ligaments, and facet joints. Used on condition pages (low back pain, neck pain, spinal stenosis) and treatment pages.

## Image Types

- Spinal column overview
- Cervical spine (neck)
- Thoracic spine (mid-back)
- Lumbar spine (low back)
- Sacrum
- Individual vertebrae cross-sections
- Intervertebral disc anatomy
- Facet joint anatomy
- Spinal ligaments
- Nerve root emergence

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Colors:** Professional medical illustration palette
- **Labeling:** Anatomical terms in English and Latin

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[region]-[structure]-[view].webp`

**Examples:**

- `lumbar-spine-anatomy.webp` - Full lumbar spine illustration
- `cervical-spine-anatomy.webp` - Full cervical spine illustration
- `lumbar-vertebra-cross-section.webp` - L4 vertebra anatomy
- `intervertebral-disc-anatomy.webp` - Disc structure
- `facet-joint-anatomy.webp` - Facet joint detail
- `lumbar-nerve-roots.webp` - Nerve root emergence
- `spinal-ligaments.webp` - Ligament anatomy
- `thoracic-spine-lateral.webp` - Side view of thoracic spine
- `sacral-spine-anatomy.webp` - Sacrum and coccyx

## Usage

These images appear on:
- Condition evaluation pages
- Treatment option pages
- Patient education hub
- Conservative care pages
