# Muscle Anatomy Images

Illustrations of muscle groups, muscle attachments, and muscular structures relevant to pain conditions.

## Purpose

Show patients the muscles involved in their pain condition or treatment, particularly core stability muscles and postural muscles. Used on exercise pages, conservative care, and physical therapy education.

## Image Types

- Core stabilizer muscles
- Paraspinal muscles
- Abdominal muscles (rectus, obliques, transverse)
- Gluteal muscles
- Hip flexors/extensors
- Shoulder girdle muscles
- Neck muscles
- Muscle fiber organization
- Motor control illustrations
- Muscle attachment points
- Muscle fatigue and tension

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Colors:** Anatomical muscle coloring (red for active, light red for relaxed)
- **Labeling:** Muscle names and attachments
- **Multiple views:** Anterior, posterior, lateral when appropriate

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[region]-[muscle-group]-[view].webp`

**Examples:**

- `core-stabilizer-muscles.webp` - All core muscles overview
- `lumbar-paraspinal-muscles.webp` - Back extensor muscles
- `rectus-abdominis.webp` - Front abdominal muscle
- `gluteus-maximus-anatomy.webp` - Gluteal muscle
- `cervical-neck-muscles.webp` - Neck muscle groups
- `hip-flexor-anatomy.webp` - Hip flexor muscles
- `shoulder-rotator-cuff.webp` - Shoulder musculature
- `lumbar-multifidus.webp` - Deep stability muscle
- `transverse-abdominis.webp` - Deep core muscle

## Usage

Used on:
- McGill Big 3 exercise page
- Conservative care pages
- Exercise demonstration pages
- Physical therapy education
- Core stability pages
