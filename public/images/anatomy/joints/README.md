# Joint Anatomy Images

Illustrations of joint structures including cartilage, synovial membrane, ligaments, and bursae.

## Purpose

Show patients the anatomy of joints relevant to pain conditions (facet joints, sacroiliac joints, shoulder joints, knee joints, etc.). Used on joint pain evaluation pages and procedure pages.

## Image Types

- Facet joint anatomy (lumbar, thoracic, cervical)
- Sacroiliac joint anatomy
- Hip joint anatomy
- Knee joint anatomy
- Shoulder joint anatomy
- Cartilage and articular surfaces
- Joint space anatomy
- Ligamentous support
- Bursa locations
- Synovial membrane structure
- Labrum anatomy

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Colors:** Professional medical illustration
- **Labeling:** Anatomical structures clearly identified
- **Cross-sections:** Show internal joint anatomy

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[joint-name]-[structure]-[view].webp`

**Examples:**

- `facet-joint-anatomy.webp` - Facet joint overview
- `lumbar-facet-joint-cross-section.webp` - Detailed cross-section
- `sacroiliac-joint-anatomy.webp` - SI joint structure
- `hip-joint-anatomy.webp` - Hip joint overview
- `knee-joint-cartilage.webp` - Knee cartilage
- `shoulder-labrum-anatomy.webp` - Shoulder labrum
- `joint-cartilage-degradation.webp` - Osteoarthritis progression
- `synovial-joint-anatomy.webp` - General joint structure
- `cervical-facet-anatomy.webp` - Neck facet joint

## Usage

Used on:
- Joint pain evaluation pages
- Facet joint injection pages
- SI joint pain pages
- Osteoarthritis education
- Conservative care pages
