# Anatomy Images

Medical illustrations of human anatomical structures used for patient education and clinical explanation.

## Overview

This folder contains detailed anatomical drawings and diagrams showing spine, nerves, joints, and muscles. Images are used to help patients understand their anatomy in relation to their pain condition.

## Subfolders

- **spine/** - Spinal column structures, vertebrae, discs
- **nerves/** - Nerve roots, peripheral nerves, dermatomes
- **joints/** - Joint structures, cartilage, synovial joints
- **muscles/** - Muscle groups, fasciae, attachments

## Global Standards

- **Format:** WEBP (preferred) or PNG (fallback)
- **Naming:** lowercase, hyphen-separated, descriptive
- **Style:** Professional medical illustration, clean, labeled
- **Accuracy:** Reviewed for anatomical correctness

## General Specifications

- **Dimensions:** Typically 800x600px to 1200x900px (responsive)
- **Resolution:** 72-150 DPI
- **Background:** Transparent (PNG) or white (WEBP)
- **Labeling:** Clear, professional medical terminology
