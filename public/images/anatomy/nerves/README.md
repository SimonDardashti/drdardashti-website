# Nerve Anatomy Images

Illustrations of the nervous system, nerve roots, peripheral nerves, and dermatome distributions.

## Purpose

Show patients how nerves exit the spine, how they distribute throughout the body, and which areas they supply. Used on radiculopathy and nerve pain pages.

## Image Types

- Nerve root emergence from spine
- Cervical nerve roots (C1-C8)
- Thoracic nerve roots (T1-T12)
- Lumbar nerve roots (L1-L5)
- Sacral nerve roots (S1-S5)
- Dermatome maps (front and back views)
- Dermatomal distributions by level
- Peripheral nerve pathways
- Upper extremity nerve distribution
- Lower extremity nerve distribution
- Brachial plexus
- Lumbar plexus

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 800x600px to 1200x900px (dermatomes often taller)
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Colors:** Color-coded by nerve level (C=blue, T=green, L=orange, S=red)
- **Labeling:** Clear nerve identification

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[region]-[nerve-type]-[view].webp`

**Examples:**

- `lumbar-nerve-roots.webp` - L1-L5 roots emerging from spine
- `cervical-nerve-roots.webp` - C1-C8 roots
- `lumbar-dermatomes-anterior.webp` - Front view dermatome map
- `lumbar-dermatomes-posterior.webp` - Back view dermatome map
- `l5-s1-dermatome.webp` - L5-S1 specific distribution
- `brachial-plexus.webp` - Cervical nerve plexus
- `lumbar-plexus.webp` - Lumbar nerve plexus
- `sciatic-nerve-pathway.webp` - Sciatic nerve path
- `radial-nerve-distribution.webp` - Upper extremity

## Usage

Used on:
- Radiculopathy pages
- Dermatome-related condition pages
- Nerve pain evaluation pages
- Treatment pages (nerve blocks, ablation)
