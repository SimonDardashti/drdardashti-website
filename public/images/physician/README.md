# Physician Images

Professional photography of Dr. Simon Dardashti, including headshots, credentials, and professional portraits.

## Purpose

Build trust and credibility through professional physician photography. Show Dr. Dardashti's expertise and approachability.

## Image Types

- Professional headshot
- Headshot variations (different angles)
- Credentials and certifications
- Clinical setting photos
- Office environment
- Certificate or diploma photos
- Professional portrait
- Casual professional photo
- Speaking/presentation photos
- Team photos if applicable

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 
  - Headshot: 600x600px to 1200x1200px (square preferred)
  - Portrait: 800x1000px to 1200x1500px (taller for full-body)
  - Professional: 1024x768px or 1200x900px
- **Format:** WEBP or PNG
- **Background:** Professional (solid color, office, or clinic setting)
- **Lighting:** Professional studio lighting
- **Clothing:** Professional medical attire
- **Quality:** High resolution, high quality

## Naming Convention

Structure: `dr-dardashti-[type]-[detail].[format]`

**Examples:**

- `dr-dardashti-headshot-professional.webp` - Main headshot
- `dr-dardashti-headshot-casual.webp` - Casual version
- `dr-dardashti-portrait-full-body.webp` - Full portrait
- `dr-dardashti-certificate-board-certified.webp` - Credential photo
- `dr-dardashti-clinical-setting.webp` - In clinic photo
- `dr-dardashti-office-professional.webp` - Office setting
- `dr-dardashti-speaking.webp` - Speaking/presentation
