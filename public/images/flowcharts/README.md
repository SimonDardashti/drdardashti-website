# Flowchart Images

Decision trees, diagnostic flowcharts, and treatment pathways as scalable vector graphics.

## Purpose

Show patients diagnostic and treatment decision processes. Help them understand how physicians evaluate pain conditions.

## Image Types

- Diagnostic decision trees
- Treatment pathway flowcharts
- When to seek medical care
- Conservative vs. interventional decision points
- Injection vs. ablation decision tree
- Medication pathway
- Specialist referral criteria
- Red flag symptom identification
- Pain assessment flowchart
- Treatment escalation pathway

## Specifications

- **Format:** SVG (scalable vector graphics) - REQUIRED
- **Dimensions:** Variable (SVG scales responsively)
- **Background:** Transparent or white
- **Colors:** Professional medical flowchart colors
- **Text:** Clear, readable fonts
- **Flow:** Top-to-bottom or left-to-right flow
- **Nodes:** Clear decision points and outcomes

## Naming Convention

Structure: `[process]-flowchart-[type].svg`

**Examples:**

- `diagnostic-flowchart-low-back-pain.svg` - Diagnostic process
- `treatment-pathway-radiculopathy.svg` - Treatment options
- `conservative-vs-interventional.svg` - Decision tree
- `when-to-seek-medical-care.svg` - Symptom guidance
- `pain-medication-pathway.svg` - Medication options
- `specialist-referral-flowchart.svg` - When to refer
- `red-flag-symptoms.svg` - Warning signs
- `injection-vs-ablation-decision.svg` - Procedure selection
- `treatment-escalation-pathway.svg` - Step-wise treatment

## Usage

Used on:
- Diagnostic pages
- Treatment decision pages
- Patient education hub
- Decision-making support pages
- Condition evaluation pages

## Technical Notes

- Use SVG format for responsive scaling
- Ensure accessibility with proper text labels
- Test rendering at various viewport sizes
- Include clear color contrast (WCAG compliant)
